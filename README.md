THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — a dark "night edition" magazine cover summing up
Tuesday, 24 November 2026. Lead story: Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as
CEO after President Trump pressed the board all weekend; shares jump 33%
and the Nasdaq rises 6%. Second story: the COP31 climate talks collapse in
Antalya after the final text drops all mention of fossil fuels. Third story:
Ukrainian sea drones sink the Russian corvette Merkury at the Benghazi naval
base in Libya. Bottom strip: 24 dead and 34 still missing after a candy
factory fire in Indonesia; Iran warns the US naval blockade may draw a
response; a magnitude 6.5 quake off Vanuatu; and a deadly storm in Brazil.

The image is drawn by a pure Python standard-library program (zlib + struct
PNG writer, hand-built 5x7 bitmap font). No network, no randomness, no
clock: two runs write byte-identical PNGs.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024 PNG, well under 1 MiB).
